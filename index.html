01 · About

Beyond Specialized Intelligence.

Toward General-Purpose AI.

I am a Software Engineering student passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and general-purpose intelligent models. My research centers on general-purpose models, model generalization, learning reliability, and intelligent systems — especially how models can acquire broader capabilities, adapt across tasks, and stay reliable in different domains.

Beyond theory, I turn ideas into practice. My projects span predictive modeling, graph neural networks, ESG analytics, autonomous driving, computer vision, and AI-powered retrieval and automation. I am drawn to building AI that is more general, adaptable, and reliable — systems that understand complex information, transfer knowledge across tasks, and keep expanding their capabilities.

I believe the future of AI lies not only in bigger or more powerful models, but in ones that are more general, reliable, and useful. My long-term goal is to explore the foundations and engineering of general-purpose AI, bridging fundamental research with real-world intelligent applications.
